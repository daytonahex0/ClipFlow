    Analytics and insights

    Learn from every clip.

    ClipFlow tracks six measures per clip, so you can see which videos hold attention and repeat what works.

    Views
    How many people the clip reached.
    Watch time
    Total minutes viewers spent on your clips.
    Completion rate
    The share of viewers who watched to the end.
    Shares
    How often viewers sent the clip to someone else.
    Saves
    How often viewers bookmarked the clip to return to it.
    Follows
    New followers gained from a clip.
    AI content insight

    How insights work

    ClipFlow compares your published clips on three things:

    • Hook style
    • Duration
    • Visual style

    It then recommends the combination that holds attention longest, so your next batch of clips starts from what already works.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    What does ClipFlow do?

    ClipFlow takes a short-form video from idea to publish-ready clip. It covers the script, storyboard, visuals, voice, edit, captions and quality control, then uses performance data to inform the next idea.

    What does the production board on this page do?

    You enter an idea and choose niche, pillar, visual style, voice tone, duration and platforms. The board writes a script outline and a timed shot list, and you move the clip through Ideas, Scripting, Producing, Review and Ready.

    Where is my board stored?

    Only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your site data, or using a private window, removes it.

    Which platforms does it support?

    Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

    How is quality scored?

    Each clip is rated from 0 to 10 on hook, story, retention, visual quality, consistency, narration, captions and call to action. The quality scorer above shows the verdict rule and the fixes for the weakest areas.

    How much does it cost?

    Pricing has not been announced. The tools on this page are free to use.
